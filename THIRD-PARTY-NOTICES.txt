ZERO C bundles the portable WinLibs GCC/MinGW-w64 toolchain in the Windows
installer. The toolchain is separate from ZERO C and includes third-party
components under their respective open-source licenses. The included WinLibs
version_info.txt identifies compiler, runtime, and tool versions. License
texts included with each component remain in the redistributed toolchain
where supplied.

WinLibs distribution and source package: https://winlibs.com/
WinLibs source repository and release archives: https://github.com/brechtsanders/winlibs_mingw
GCC source and license information: https://gcc.gnu.org/
MinGW-w64 source and license information: https://www.mingw-w64.org/
GNU Binutils source and license information: https://www.gnu.org/software/binutils/
GNU GDB source and license information: https://www.gnu.org/software/gdb/

ZERO C is a separate application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
the GCC, MinGW-w64, or WinLibs projects.
